# Community Sector Funding Tracker

Purpose:

Track Commonwealth community sector funding over time.

Key questions:

1. Has baseline funding increased?
2. How much funding came through indexation?
3. How much funding came through supplementation?
4. Has funding kept pace with CPI?
5. Has funding kept pace with SCHADS?
6. What is the cumulative funding gap?
